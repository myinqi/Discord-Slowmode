Counting rules

  • Each Discord identity counts once per Suno UUID, regardless of how many emojis or Discord posts it used.
  • The author of a song post is excluded from that post's rating.
  • Authenticated reactions from all Player variants are included and linked to their Discord identity.
  • Anonymous Player reactions use the bot identity and therefore count at most once per song.
  • Historical snapshots include songs posted on that date and reactions still recorded up to the end of that day. Reactions removed before this feature was introduced cannot be reconstructed.
